Using WordNest

How do the cards work?

Tap a card to flip between French and English. Swipe right (or tap Got it) when you know the word, and left (or Again) to keep practising it. Swipe down on the card to show the example sentence, and up to hide it.

What's the difference between Cards and Quiz?

Every collection has a Cards / Quiz switch under its title. Cards is flashcard practice. Quiz is 10 questions mixing le or la, fill the blank, build the sentence from word tiles, and multiple choice. Right answers count a word as mastered; misses send it back to practise.

How does the streak work?

Finish one session (cards or quiz) in a day and that day counts. Tap the 🔥 chip to see your streak, your best run and this week.

Can I change my daily goal or redo the setup questions?

Yes. Tap the 🔥 chip, then Preferences → Redo setup. Your progress is kept.

How do I start over?

Tap the 🔥 chip and choose Reset mastered words, then tap again to confirm. Your streak is kept.

Troubleshooting

I don't hear the pronunciation

Turn up the volume and check your device isn't muted. WordNest uses your device's own voices, so make sure a French voice is installed: on iPhone and iPad, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French. On Android, check Settings → Text-to-speech.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved only on the device you use. It doesn't move between your phone and computer, and it's removed if you delete the app or clear your browser's site data.

I spotted a wrong word or translation

Thank you! Email the French word and what looks off, and it'll be fixed in the next update.
