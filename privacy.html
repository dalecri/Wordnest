Privacy policy

WordNest is a French flashcard app for iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version: WordNest collects nothing.

No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. WordNest does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no servers of its own and makes no network requests while you use it: all of the words, examples, fonts and images are built into the app.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, WordNest saves a small amount of data in the app's local storage on your device:

  • which words you've marked as mastered,
  • the days you completed a session (for your streak),
  • your setup choices: why you're learning, your level, cards per day, and Quebec or France French.

This data never leaves your device. We can't see it, and WordNest doesn't back it up or sync it. If you use the web version, it's kept in that browser only.

Pronunciation audio

When you tap the speaker button, the word is read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech voice (on iPhone and iPad, Apple's speech system). WordNest doesn't record audio, doesn't use the microphone, and doesn't send words anywhere to be spoken.

Deleting your data

  • To reset your progress, open the streak sheet (tap the 🔥 chip) and choose Reset mastered words.
  • To remove everything, delete the app. On the web, clear the site data for WordNest in your browser settings.

Children

WordNest is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

WordNest contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. If you download it from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may collect information under their own privacy policies; WordNest receives none of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. WordNest will never start collecting data without saying so here first.
